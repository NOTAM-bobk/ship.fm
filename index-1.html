About snip.fm

A tiny music discovery feed. Scroll, hear 30 seconds, heart or save what sticks.

Where the clips come from

This build plays 30-second previews and artwork from Apple's iTunes Search API, so it runs with no keys. The iHeartRadio source is a drop-in provider in the code.
